Consent & Disclosure

Before we begin, please review the following

1. Nature of this service

OPENCARE is a supportive wellbeing tool designed to help you notice and respond to shifts in stress and mood. It is not a licensed medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, or cure any mental health condition. Nothing generated inside this pod, including AI chatbot responses, biometric readouts, or guided exercises, should be treated as a substitute for professional medical, psychological, or psychiatric advice.

2. Data we collect

To calibrate the pod to you, we collect the information you provide during profile creation (name, age, self-reported habits and notes), a photo taken via your device's camera, your answers to a short preference quiz, and session data generated while you are inside the pod, including simulated stress-index readings, breathing exercise completion, and any messages exchanged with the AI chatbot. This data is used to personalise your session and, where you engage a human professional, may be shared with that provider to inform your consultation.

3. Limitations of AI-based support

The AI chatbot available inside the pod is an automated system. It can offer general, supportive conversation and suggest exercises such as guided breathing, but it cannot assess risk, provide a diagnosis, or respond to a medical or psychiatric emergency. If at any point you feel you are in crisis or at risk of harming yourself or others, please exit the pod immediately and contact local emergency services or a crisis helpline.

4. Escalation and human professionals

Access to a human mental health professional through this pod may be offered based on your session's simulated stress and wellbeing readings. Where your readings do not meet the threshold for automatic escalation, you may still be offered the option to connect with a professional for a predetermined fee. Choosing to pay for this access is entirely voluntary, and declining it will not affect your ability to use the AI chatbot or any other feature of the pod.

5. Voluntary participation

Your participation in this session is entirely voluntary. You may exit the pod, stop any exercise, or end a conversation with the AI chatbot at any time, for any reason, without needing to provide justification. Declining consent below will prevent you from proceeding further.

6. Data retention & withdrawal of consent

Session data, including your profile details and photo, is retained only for as long as is necessary to provide this service, unless you have engaged a human professional, in which case relevant records may be retained in line with that provider's own record-keeping obligations. You may request the deletion of your data or withdraw your consent at any time by contacting OPENCARE support.

7. Camera & photo capture consent

As part of setting up your profile, OPENCARE will prompt your device to open its camera so you can take a single identity photo. The captured image is used only to personalise and verify your session and is handled under the same data retention terms described above.

8. Acknowledgement

By checking the box below and then pressing the verification control, you confirm that you have read and understood this disclosure, that you specifically consent to OPENCARE capturing your photo, that you are voluntarily choosing to proceed, and that you understand OPENCARE is a supportive tool and not a replacement for professional or emergency care.
